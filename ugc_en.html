About Me

I make brand content feel less like an ad.

My approach combines natural delivery, clean visuals, real-life scenarios, and direct messaging. The goal is simple: make the viewer feel like they are discovering a product through someone they trust.

I handle concepting, scripting, filming, editing, captions, B-roll, and final delivery, with versions optimized for organic posting or paid social campaigns.
