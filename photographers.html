About me

Images that feel lived, not staged.

I’m Omar Nasser, an independent photographer and visual storyteller working between Cairo and Dubai. My work sits between documentary honesty and editorial precision — from intimate portraits and weddings to hospitality, architecture and destination stories.

I’m most interested in the small details that make a frame memorable: the way a hand rests, the quiet before a room fills, late-afternoon light moving across a wall, or the expression that happens just after the posed photograph.
